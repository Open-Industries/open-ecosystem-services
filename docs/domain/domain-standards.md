# Domain Standards — Ecosystem Services

Alignment mapping for this core. *Aligned to* throughout: SEEA EA accounts compiled from
this core still follow the adopter's national-accounts and verification processes.

## Concept mapping

| Platform concept | Domain standard | Mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Ecosystem assets | UN SEEA EA (ecosystem extent accounts) | Asset registry by type, area and region |
| Condition & monitoring | UN SEEA EA (condition accounts) | Monitoring stations and health scoring |
| Service capacity & issuance | UN SEEA EA (ecosystem services accounts) | Guarded capacity accounts; the no-overrun invariant |
| Valuation | ISO 14008 | Valuation rules with value/uncertainty envelopes and reasoned decisions |
| Carbon service | ISO 14064 family | Carbon as one service type among water, biodiversity, pollination |

## Scope boundaries

This core does not certify credits or operate a registry of record for any carbon or
biodiversity standard; issuance here is the internal accounting step before external
registry submission.
